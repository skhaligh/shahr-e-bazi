How to play

🕹️Roll: put your thumb anywhere on the left half of the screen and slide it where you want to go.
🟢Jump: tap the JUMP button.
✌️Double jump: tap JUMP again while you are in the air (after World 1).
🪂Glide: hold JUMP while you fall (after World 2).
💨Dash: tap DASH for a burst of speed (after World 3).
⏸️Pause: tap ❚❚ at the top.
🪜Ladders: roll up to one and push the stick up to climb. Pull down to slide, JUMP to leap off.
🌀Pipes: roll into a glowing ring to whoosh through the tunnel.
🌬Fans: roll into the air column and let it lift you.
🪙Coins: spend them in the Shop on skins, extra lives and a magnet.
💖Hearts: every world has a friend to meet and a kind choice to make. Collect Hearts for a glowing aura and a secret world!
🪨Wisdom Stones: roll past one and a little piece of wisdom appears at the top.
